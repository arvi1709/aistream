# AutoStream – Product Knowledge Base

## About AutoStream

AutoStream is a SaaS platform that provides automated video editing tools for content creators.
It is designed to help creators quickly turn raw footage into polished content for platforms
like YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok.

AutoStream focuses on:
- Speeding up video production
- Reducing manual editing effort
- Helping creators publish consistently

---

## Pricing & Plans

### Basic Plan
- $29 per month  
- Up to 10 videos per month  
- 720p video resolution  

Best for small creators who publish occasionally and want affordable automation.

### Pro Plan
- $79 per month  
- Unlimited videos  
- 4K video resolution  
- AI-generated captions  

Best for professional creators and teams who publish frequently and need high-quality output.

---

## Company Policies

- No refunds are available after 7 days from purchase.
- 24/7 customer support is available only on the Pro plan.
